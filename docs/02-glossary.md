# Glossary

Every new term, in one simple line. Add a term here the first time it appears anywhere in the book.

- **WER (Word Error Rate):** the share of words the speech model got wrong.
- **Reference:** the correct text, what the person really said.
- **Hypothesis:** the text the model wrote.
- **Substitution, deletion, insertion:** the three kinds of word mistakes. A wrong word, a missing word, an extra word.
- **Edit distance:** the fewest word edits needed to turn one text into another.
- **Normalization:** cleaning two texts (case, punctuation, Unicode form) so they can be compared fairly.
- **Unicode mark:** a character that attaches to another one, like Hindi vowel signs.
